The short version: The launcher keeps most of your data on your own computer. When you use online features like friends, chat, communities, and voice chat, our servers handle your Minecraft username, your Minecraft ID, your IP address, and what you send. Messages are deleted after 3 days, and live chat after 1 hour. A computer checks messages for slurs and hate speech as they are sent, and live chat for swearing. We do not save or read everyone’s messages. Only a message that breaks the rules is kept, so we can deal with it. If you report someone, we keep your report and any screenshots or clips you attach until we delete them. We never see your Microsoft password. Voice chat is never recorded. We do not sell your information. Ads on our website and in the launcher come from ad networks that collect some data of their own.

1. What this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains what information SolarClient (“SolarClient”, “we”, “us”) collects, why we collect it, and what we do with it. It covers our website at figgysmp.shop, the SolarClient launcher on every platform and release channel, and the SolarClient online features. By using SolarClient, you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

2. Information kept on your computer

Most of what the launcher uses never leaves your computer.

  • Sign in. You sign in on Microsoft’s own sign in page. Microsoft then gives the launcher tokens that let it start Minecraft for you. These tokens are saved on your computer and are only sent to Microsoft and Mojang. We never see your Microsoft password, and our servers never receive these tokens.
  • Settings and game files. Your settings, game installs, mods, themes, and layouts are saved on your computer.
  • Your agreement to the Terms. The launcher remembers which version of the Terms you agreed to, so it only asks again when they change.
  • Message history. The launcher saves your direct, group, and community messages on your computer so you can scroll back through them. It deletes them after 3 days, the same as our servers.
  • A ban, if you have one. If you are banned, the launcher saves the ban details so it stays locked until the ban ends.
  • Logs and crash reports. These stay on your computer. We never collect them automatically. If you choose to send us one for help, we use it only to fix your problem.

You can remove all of this by uninstalling SolarClient and deleting its data folder.

3. Information our servers receive

SolarClient runs its own servers for online features and updates. This is what they handle.

3.1 Your Minecraft identity

When the launcher connects, it proves that you own your Minecraft account using Mojang’s official login check. Our server then receives your Minecraft username and your Minecraft ID (called a UUID). We save these, along with the last time you were online, so your friends can find you.

3.2 Your connection

Our server sees your IP address when you connect. We record it in a server log together with your username, to protect the service and to stop abuse. We do not use it to work out where you live, and we do not combine it with other data to build a profile of you.

The launcher also sends a device code. This is a one way code made from your computer’s built in ID (on Windows, the machine ID that Windows creates when it is installed). It cannot be turned back into that ID, and it contains nothing about you, your files, or your hardware. We keep the codes for the last few computers each account used, and we use them for one thing only: making a ban apply to the computer as well as the account, so a banned player cannot just make a new account.

3.3 Friends and status

We save who you follow and who you block. While you are online, your friends can see your status and, if you choose to share it, what you are playing. You can hide your status in the launcher.

3.4 Messages

  • Direct messages and group messages are passed straight to the people they are meant for. If someone is offline, we hold the message until they next connect, and then delete it from our server once it is delivered. If they do not come back within 3 days, the message is deleted anyway. Group details, such as the group name and its members, are saved.
  • Community messages are saved on our server so members can read them later, for 3 days. After 3 days they are deleted. We also keep no more than the latest 200 messages in each text channel, and all of a channel’s messages are deleted when the channel or the community is deleted. Community names, channel names, and member lists are saved too.
  • Live chat is kept only in our server’s memory, never saved to disk. Messages disappear after 1 hour, no more than the last 100 are kept, and they are all erased whenever the server restarts.

We delete old messages to save space on our servers. The launcher shows a note next to each chat box so you know.

3.5 The chat safety filter and flagged messages

To keep chat safe, our server automatically checks each message as it is sent, in direct messages, group chats, communities, and live chat. The check is done by a computer, not a person. Everywhere, it looks for slurs, hate speech, and telling someone to kill or hurt themselves, including attempts to sneak those past the filter with symbols, spaces, or look alike letters. In live chat only, it also looks for swear words and their abbreviations, because live chat is public. Swearing in direct messages, group chats, and communities is not flagged.

  • We are not saving or reading everyone’s messages. A message that passes the check is handled exactly as described above and nothing extra is kept.
  • A message that fails the check is not sent. You are told it was not sent. A copy is saved so that we can decide on consequences, such as a warning, a mute, or a ban. The copy includes the message, your Minecraft username and ID, where you sent it (for example live chat, or a direct message to a named friend), and when.
  • Only the two SolarClient owners can see flagged messages. Our server checks their Minecraft accounts every time, and nobody else can open them.
  • Flagged messages are not deleted automatically. They are kept until one of the owners deletes them, so that repeat problems can be dealt with fairly.

3.6 Reports

When you report another player from the launcher, our server saves the report so the SolarClient owners can review it:

  • your Minecraft username and ID, and the reported player’s username and ID;
  • the reason you picked, what you wrote, and when you sent it;
  • the message you reported, if you reported one from live chat, a DM, or a group chat (the launcher adds it for you, so you can see it before you send); and
  • any screenshots or videos you choose to attach. Files are checked to make sure they really are images or videos, and they are stored on our server, not shared with anyone else.

Only the two SolarClient owners can see reports. The reported player is not told who reported them. Reports and their files are kept until an owner deletes them. If attachments ever take up too much space, the oldest attachments are deleted first. Please do not attach anything showing other people’s private information unless it is the problem you are reporting.

3.7 Voice chat

Your voice is sent live through our server to the other people in the same voice channel. We never record it or store it. Noise cancellation runs on your own computer. Members of a community can see who is in its voice channels, and the people in a voice channel can see when you are muted or deafened.

3.9 Moderation records and bans

We save staff roles and chat mutes so that moderation keeps working. A mute records your Minecraft username and ID, the reason, when it started and ends, and which owner issued it, and it is removed when it ends or is lifted. If you are banned, we save your Minecraft username and ID, the reason, when the ban started and when it ends (or that it is permanent), which owner issued it, a short ban ID, and the device codes of the computers your account used. The ban is removed from our records when it ends or when an owner lifts it.

When an owner bans, unbans, mutes, or unmutes someone, the same details (but never device codes or IP addresses) are posted to a private staff channel on Discord, so we have a record of every decision. Discord handles that information under the Discord Privacy Policy.

3.10 Updates and player count

To check for updates and to show how many people are online, the launcher contacts our server and GitHub. Like any internet request, these include your IP address and basic technical details.

4. Our website

Our website has no sign up and no accounts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log your IP address and browser details when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The agreement box next to the download button is not saved and is never sent to us.

Our website shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technology to show and measure ads, which can include ads based on your visits to our website and other websites. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ad cookies are set. You can turn off personalized ads at Google My Ad Center, and you can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Our Cookie Policy has more detail.

5. Ads in the launcher

The launcher shows ads from third party ad networks, including Adsterra. These ads load from the ad network’s own servers, which receive your IP address and basic device and browser details, and may use cookies inside the launcher to count views, limit repeats, and prevent fraud. We do not give ad networks your Minecraft username, your messages, or anything else from your account.

6. Other services the launcher connects to

To do its job, the launcher connects straight from your computer to the services below. Each one has its own privacy policy.

  • Microsoft and Xbox, to sign you in.
  • Mojang, to download Minecraft, check your account, and load skins.
  • Modrinth, to search for and download mods, resource packs, and shaders.
  • Fabric, Quilt, and Forge, to download mod loaders.
  • Eclipse Adoptium, to download Java.
  • mc-heads.net and Crafatar, to show player heads and skins.
  • GitHub, to download launcher updates.
  • Discord, only if Discord is running on your computer, to show that you are playing Minecraft on your Discord profile. This talks to the Discord app on your own computer.

7. How we use information

We use the information above only to:

  • run SolarClient and its features;
  • deliver your messages and voice;
  • keep SolarClient safe, stop spam and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service and Rules;
  • fix problems; and
  • follow the law.

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it:

  • with the people you choose to talk to;
  • with the companies that host our servers, only so they can run them;
  • with Discord, only the ban and mute details described in section 3.9, in a private staff channel;
  • when the law requires it, or when we need to protect someone’s safety; and
  • if SolarClient is ever sold or transferred, in which case this policy keeps applying to your information.

8. How long we keep it

  • Minecraft username, ID, and last online time: for as long as you use online features. We may remove accounts that have not been used for a long time.
  • Follows, blocks, and group details: until you change them or ask us to delete them.
  • Messages held for offline friends: until they are delivered, and never more than 3 days.
  • Community messages: 3 days, and no more than the latest 200 in each text channel.
  • Live chat: 1 hour, in memory only, gone sooner if the server restarts.
  • Flagged messages: until one of the SolarClient owners deletes them.
  • Reports and their attachments: until one of the SolarClient owners deletes them. The oldest attachments are deleted first if they take up too much space.
  • Chat mutes: until the mute ends or is lifted.
  • Bans and their device codes: until the ban ends or is lifted. Device codes for accounts that are not banned are replaced as you use new computers, and only the last few are kept.
  • Voice: never stored.
  • Server logs with IP addresses: only as long as we need them for security, and then deleted.

9. Security

We protect our servers with account checks through Mojang, limits on how much data anyone can send, and other measures. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise that your information will never be accessed without permission. Do not share passwords, personal details, or anything else sensitive in chat or voice.
