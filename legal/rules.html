The short version: Be decent to people. No swearing in live chat, because everyone can see it. In DMs and group chats you can swear. Slurs, hate speech, harassment, and telling people to hurt themselves are never allowed anywhere, and neither is trying to sneak them past the filter. Break the rules and you can be muted or banned, and a ban locks the whole launcher on your account and your computer.

These rules apply everywhere in SolarClient where you can talk to people: live chat, direct messages, group chats, communities, and voice chat. They are part of our Terms of Service.

1. No swearing in live chat

  • Live chat is one public room for everyone on SolarClient, including younger players, so keep it clean.
  • That means no swear words, and no abbreviations or dodges for them either: things like “wtf”, “stfu”, “f*ck”, “sh1t” or splitting a word up with spaces all count.
  • Live chat checks every message before it sends. A message with swearing in it does not go through, and it is saved for the SolarClient team. Doing it again and again can get you muted.

2. Swearing in DMs and group chats is fine, attacking people is not

  • In direct messages, group chats, and communities you can swear. “This game is so damn hard” is fine.
  • Swearing at someone to bully or hurt them is not. Insulting the same person again and again, ganging up on someone, or following them around chats to keep at them counts as harassment.
  • Friendly trash talk between friends is fine. If someone asks you to stop, stop.

3. No slurs or hate speech, anywhere

  • No racial slurs, homophobic or transphobic slurs, or slurs about disability or religion. Not as a joke, not “ironically”, not in a username or a channel name.
  • No attacking or mocking people because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar traits.
  • No praising hate groups, Nazis, terrorists, or violence against any group of people.
  • Getting around the filter counts the same as saying it. Writing a slur with symbols, numbers, spaces, dots, stretched letters, or look alike letters from other alphabets is treated exactly like writing it normally.

4. No threats, and never tell anyone to hurt themselves

  • No threats of violence, even as a joke.
  • Never tell anyone to kill themselves or hurt themselves (“kys” included).
  • If someone seems to be in danger, report it or tell us on the SolarClient Discord. If it is an emergency, contact your local emergency number.

5. Keep it safe for everyone

  • No sexual content, and absolutely nothing that sexualizes anyone under 18. We report that to the authorities.
  • No gore or shock content.
  • No sharing anyone’s private information, such as their real name, address, school, phone number, photos, or IP address, without their permission. This is called doxxing and it gets a ban.
  • Do not ask people your age or younger for personal details or photos, and do not try to move them to private apps.

6. No spam, scams, or harmful links

  • No flooding chat, repeating the same message, or mass messaging people.
  • No scams, fake giveaways, phishing links, or “free Minecraft account” offers.
  • No links to malware, cheats, cracked launchers, or anything meant to harm someone’s computer or account.
  • No advertising other launchers, servers, or products without our permission.

7. Be honest about who you are

  • Do not pretend to be SolarClient staff, SHARK_TG, Figgy_1234, or anyone else.
  • Do not use someone else’s account, or share yours.

8. Voice chat

  • All of the rules above apply to voice too, except that swearing is fine in voice the same way it is in DMs.
  • No mic spam, soundboards blasting at people, or deliberately loud noise.
  • Do not record people without asking everyone in the call first.

9. Do not mess with SolarClient itself

  • No trying to break, crash, or overload our servers, and no bots or modified launchers on our online features.
  • No trying to get into the moderation tools or anyone else’s account.
  • No ban evasion. If you are banned, do not come back on another account or another computer. That makes the ban longer, usually permanent.

10. Reporting someone

  • Use the Report button: hover a live chat message and click the flag, or right click a player in live chat, the online list, or your friends list, or open the menu on a message in a DM or group chat.
  • Pick what happened, tell us what they did, and add screenshots or short clips if you have them (images and videos, up to 4 files).
  • Only report things that actually break the rules. Someone swearing in a DM or group chat is allowed, so please do not report it unless it is racist, hateful, harassment, a threat, or something else on this page.
  • Only the SolarClient team sees reports, and the player is not told who reported them. Making false reports on purpose, or flooding us with reports, breaks these rules too.

11. What happens if you break the rules

  • The message does not send. Messages with slurs, hate speech, or telling someone to hurt themselves are stopped everywhere, and swearing is stopped in live chat. Those messages are kept so the owners can review them. We do not keep or read everyone else’s messages. See our Privacy Policy.
  • Mute. You can be muted in live chat, for a set time or permanently. The launcher tells you how long.
  • Temporary ban. From a few minutes up to years.
  • Permanent ban. For serious or repeated rule breaking.

A ban locks the launcher on your account and on the computers your account used. You cannot use the launcher, start games from it, or use chat, friends, communities, or voice until it ends. The launcher shows you the reason, how long the ban is, and when it ends. We can skip straight to a ban for anything serious.

12. Appeals

If you think a mute or ban was a mistake, message us on the SolarClient Discord. Include your Minecraft username and, for a ban, the ban ID shown in the launcher.
